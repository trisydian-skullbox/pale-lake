## What Even Is This

A horror romance dating sim set at a cursed woodland cabin resort on the shore of a living, hungry lake. Six romanceable staff members. A photographer protagonist who arrives and cannot leave. A camera that sees more than it should. Something ancient and hungry at the bottom of the water that has been waiting a very long time for someone like you.

Also there is graphic violence and graphic sexual content and boy-love coded characters approachable from any gender perspective because life is too short not to explore the voices in your head.

You've been warned. You're still here. Good.

---

## How Did This Happen

I am Trisydian and I am responsible for this because I have no self-regulatory mechanisms to stop myself from achieving the weirdness that lives in my skull.

The longer version: I went down a rabbit hole building AI chatbots and lore worlds because I am a chaos goblin with a hyperfixation problem. Somewhere between writing character bibles and lorebooks I looked up and realised I wasn't building chatbots anymore... I was building a dating sim. I was already halfway there. I love dating sims. I have never made a game before in my life.

Naturally the only reasonable response was to commit fully and make the most unhinged horror romance I could think of.

Here we are.

---

## Why This Game Specifically

I wanted to play a game with boy-love coded characters that could be approached from a male or female perspective. I wanted graphic horror that kept everybody on their toes. I wanted romance that earned it and didn't apologise for where it went. I wanted BOTH! The graphic violence AND the graphic NSFW content in the same game, treated with equal weight.

I haven't found that game. So I'm making it.

## This Repository

This repo contains the promotional website for Checkout Never: Pale Lake. A single page HTML site built without frameworks or dependencies. Just HTML, CSS, and chaos goblin energy.

### Viewing Locally

No installation required. It's just a webpage. You can do this.

1. Clone or download this repository
2. Open `index.html` in any modern browser
3. That's genuinely it
```bash
git clone https://github.com/trisydian-skullbox/pale-lake.git
cd pale-lake
open index.html
```
